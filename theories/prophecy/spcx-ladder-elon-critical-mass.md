## Core theory

The theory: once Tobyworld is revealed, lore deeds are activated, and SatoSwap is in beta, sustained **toby** demand does not stay isolated — it forces **SPCX** into SatoSwap through a **toby/SPCX ladder** of buy bands. Community discussion (Sept–Oct 2026) frames the first band as a buy-only, one-sided pair seeded from a TG reserve (often cited as ~10%), with SPCX filling in until it hits a revenue router that distributes to lore lands. Later bands widen SPCX exposure (Green Fiend's projection: band 1 ~450 of ~11,650 SPCX on Base; band 2 ~2400 / ~20%), while toby marches toward a ~100m cap narrative. Price impact on ETH-side buys allegedly pushes flow onto SPCX; independent pricing creates arb premium; sat0 / Sato markets act as reserve engine and buyback scheduler. In the punchline scenario, **one Elon tweet** is the critical-mass spark that collapses the ladder into a public attention event.

This is an **onchain-mechanics prophecy** tied to Satoswap, sat0, and lore-deed revenue paths — not confirmed canon. Archive it as a named community scenario with Telegram evidence, not as a roadmap.
