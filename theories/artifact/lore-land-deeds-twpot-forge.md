## Core theory

**Lore Land Deeds** are Tobyworld's production land NFTs — onchain as the Lore / LORE collection (chat name: ProductionNFT2) — minted during a timed **TWPOT Genesis Forge**. Community contract reads describe an ERC-721 with partitioned supply (commonly cited 2,500 community / 1,000 treasury / 500 AMM = 4,000), a once-bound Genesis Forge as community minter, and ERC-6551 token-bound accounts so each deed can act as a portable inventory/identity container. Forging required burning **Taboshi** and paying **Patience** (often reported as 50 TABOSHI + a rising Patience schedule, e.g. 20 early), with a short fair-access rate limit (~2 deeds / frog / 24h) and an announced ~5-day window.

The forge was foreshadowed in mid-2025 Patience notes that already named lore land deeds as a Patience use. Days before go-live, chat parsed a forge riddle (Patience + leaf → forge). On **2026-08-12** the mechanic went live; by **2026-08-16–17** toad-dev announced the forge growing quiet, an onchain ownership snapshot, and automatic one-for-one **canonical Lore Land Deed** delivery to snapshot holders — no claim, no gas — with a warning not to trade the Genesis Lore during passage. The Genesis NFT remains a historical forge record; rights continue on the canonical deed.

What deeds do after awakening is partly sealed by later toad-dev language (doors, keepers, forge → land → sat0) and partly still community inference (yield flywheels, tenancy, validators). Archive the **artifact + forge ritual** as community-documented with Toadgod-adjacent confirmation of close/migration; keep yield/tenancy/later-epoch forges as marked speculation. Do not collapse the 2024 Zora `twpot` / Taboshi collection into this 2026 Genesis Forge land-deed event.
